## Overview

**Virat Kohli** (born 5 November 1988) is an Indian international
cricketer and former captain of the Indian national cricket team. He is
a right-handed top-order batter and occasional right-arm medium-pace
bowler.

Kohli is widely regarded as one of the leading batters of his
generation, particularly in limited-overs cricket. His career includes
major international titles, extensive individual records, long-term
leadership of India, and a sustained career with Royal Challengers
Bengaluru in the Indian Premier League (IPL).

## Early Life

Kohli was born in New Delhi and spent his formative years in Uttam
Nagar. He attended Vishal Bharti Public School and showed an interest in
cricket from a very young age.

In 1998, the West Delhi Cricket Academy was established, and Kohli began
formal cricket training. Rajkumar Sharma became an important coach in
his development.

Kohli initially faced difficulties getting selected for Delhi's Under-14
side but subsequently progressed through age-group cricket. He later
moved to Saviour Convent School to support his cricketing development.

A major personal turning point occurred in December 2006 when his
father, Prem Nath Kohli, died. Kohli continued playing and subsequently
committed himself even more strongly to cricket.

## Youth Career

### Delhi Age-Group Cricket

Kohli progressed through Delhi's junior teams and produced increasingly
strong performances.

Notable milestones included:

-   Early success in Under-15 cricket.
-   Leadership of the Delhi Under-15 side.
-   Strong performances in the Vijay Merchant Trophy with the Delhi
    Under-17 team.
-   A major 2005--06 season in which he became the leading scorer of the
    tournament.
-   List A debut in February 2006.
-   First-class debut for Delhi in the 2006 Ranji Trophy season.

During his early first-class career, one of his most remembered
performances came immediately after his father's death, when he returned
to the match and scored 90 against Karnataka.

## India Under-19 Career

Kohli represented India Under-19 on tours to England, Pakistan and New
Zealand and developed a reputation for technically sound batting against
both pace and spin.

### 2008 Under-19 World Cup

One of the defining milestones of Kohli's youth career was captaining
India to victory in the **2008 ICC Under-19 World Cup in Malaysia**.

He scored 235 runs in the tournament and contributed important
performances with both bat and ball. His century against West Indies
Under-19 was one of the tournament's standout innings.

The Under-19 World Cup victory became a major stepping stone toward his
senior international career.

## International Career

### ODI Debut and Early Years

Kohli made his ODI debut for India in August 2008 against Sri Lanka. He
initially opened the innings because of injuries to senior players.

His early international position was not completely secure, but strong
performances in domestic and emerging-player cricket helped him
establish himself.

In 2009, he scored his maiden ODI century against Sri Lanka.

By 2010, he had become a regular part of India's ODI setup and was
appointed vice-captain for a tri-series in Zimbabwe.

### 2011 Cricket World Cup

Kohli played an important role in India's **2011 Cricket World Cup**
victory.

He scored a century in his World Cup debut and also contributed in the
final against Sri Lanka as India won the tournament.

### Test Debut

Kohli made his Test debut against West Indies in June 2011.

After an initially difficult period, he established himself in Test
cricket and scored his maiden Test century in Adelaide in January 2012.

### Breakthrough in Limited-Overs Cricket

The 2012 period was especially significant for Kohli's ODI career.

Important innings included:

-   An unbeaten 133 against Sri Lanka in Hobart while chasing a large
    target.
-   A career-best 183 against Pakistan in the 2012 Asia Cup.

These performances strengthened his reputation as one of the world's
leading chase specialists.

## Rise as a Leader

By 2013, Kohli had become a central member of India's batting order and
was increasingly trusted with leadership responsibilities.

He contributed to India's **2013 Champions Trophy** victory and later
became a regular captain across formats.

After MS Dhoni's retirement from Test cricket during India's 2014--15
Australia tour, Kohli became India's full-time Test captain.

His first series as permanent Test captain included three centuries in
his first three innings in Australia.

## Peak Years: 2016--2018

The period from 2016 to 2018 is widely regarded as one of the strongest
phases of Kohli's career.

Highlights included:

-   Player of the Tournament at the 2016 ICC World Twenty20.
-   Multiple Test double-centuries.
-   Becoming the fastest player to several major ODI run milestones.
-   Reaching the No. 1 position in Test batting rankings.
-   Leading India to its first Test series victory in Australia.
-   Becoming one of the most successful Test captains in Indian cricket.

In 2018, Kohli became the first Indian batter to hold the No. 1 ranking
in all three international formats at different points in his career.

## Captaincy

Kohli was one of India's most prominent modern captains.

His captaincy era was associated with:

-   A strong emphasis on fitness and athletic fielding.
-   Greater importance placed on fast bowling depth.
-   Strong performances in overseas Test cricket.
-   India's first Test series win in Australia.
-   Consistent competitiveness across international formats.

He stepped down from T20I, ODI and Test captaincy roles in succession
during the 2021--2022 period.

## 2022--2024 Resurgence

After an extended period without an international century, Kohli
returned to major form during the **2022 Asia Cup**, scoring his first
T20I century.

At the 2022 T20 World Cup, he was India's leading run-scorer and
produced the famous unbeaten 82 against Pakistan.

### 2023 Cricket World Cup

The 2023 ODI World Cup was another defining chapter.

Kohli:

-   Scored 765 runs in the tournament.
-   Was named Player of the Tournament.
-   Scored his 50th ODI century.
-   Surpassed Sachin Tendulkar's previous record for ODI centuries.

## 2024 T20 World Cup

Kohli had a relatively modest tournament by his usual standards but
delivered in the final against South Africa.

He scored 76 and was named Player of the Match as India won the **2024
ICC Men's T20 World Cup**.

Following the victory, Kohli announced his retirement from T20I cricket.

## 2025 Champions Trophy

Kohli played an important role in India's **2025 ICC Champions Trophy**
campaign.

The referenced article records that he scored a century during the
tournament and became the fastest player to reach 14,000 ODI runs. India
went on to win the title.

## Test Retirement

On 12 May 2025, Kohli announced his retirement from Test cricket at age
36.

His Test career ended after 123 matches, 9,230 runs and 30 centuries
according to the statistics displayed on the referenced Wikipedia page.

## Franchise Career --- Royal Challengers Bengaluru

Kohli has been associated with Royal Challengers Bengaluru since the
inaugural IPL era.

He became one of the defining players and long-term faces of the
franchise.

His IPL career includes:

-   Multiple seasons as RCB captain.
-   Major run-scoring seasons.
-   IPL Orange Cap awards in 2016 and 2024.
-   IPL runner-up finishes in 2009, 2011 and 2016.
-   IPL titles listed for 2025 and 2026 on the referenced page.

Kohli is also described as the leading run-scorer in IPL history.

## Playing Style

Kohli is primarily a right-handed top-order batter.

His batting is characterized by:

-   Strong technique.
-   Excellent running between the wickets.
-   Ability to rotate strike consistently.
-   Aggressive stroke play when required.
-   Strong performance in run chases.
-   High concentration and competitive intensity.
-   Adaptability between ODI, Test and T20 formats.

He is also known for his fitness standards and athletic fielding.

## Aggression and Competitive Personality

Kohli's on-field intensity has been a prominent part of his public
image.

His aggressive style is often associated with:

-   Strong emotional involvement in matches.
-   High competitiveness.
-   Visible celebrations.
-   Direct interaction with opponents.
-   Strong emphasis on physical fitness and preparation.

This intensity has contributed to both his popularity and debate around
his playing persona.

## Legacy

Virat Kohli's career is notable for the combination of longevity,
consistency, leadership and statistical achievements.

His legacy is particularly strong in ODI cricket, where his run-scoring
record and performances in successful chases have made him one of the
most celebrated batters of the modern era.

His international career also includes participation in multiple World
Cup and Champions Trophy victories, while his long association with
Royal Challengers Bengaluru has made him one of the most recognizable
figures in the IPL.
